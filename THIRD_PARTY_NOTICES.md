# Third-party sources and reuse boundaries

## Published measurements

Lee JC et al. *Regulatory T cell control of systemic immunity and immunotherapy response in liver metastasis*. Science Immunology 5(52), eaba0759 (2020).

- [Original paper and DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciimmunol.aba0759)
- [Author manuscript in PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7755924/)
- [BioStudies archive S-EPMC7755924](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/studies/S-EPMC7755924)
- [Table S1 workbook](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/files/S-EPMC7755924/NIHMS1640106-supplement-Table_1.xlsx)

The complete workbook, article text and original figure images are not included. `observations.csv` contains an attributed factual extract of selected endpoint values with source-cell coordinates. `analysis.json` and `data.js` contain that extract and calculated descriptive results. `PROVENANCE.json` records the source identifier, size and checksum.

## Software dependency

The analysis uses [openpyxl](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/), pinned to version 3.1.5 in `requirements.txt`. It is installed separately, not vendored here. Its own license and notices apply to that package.
